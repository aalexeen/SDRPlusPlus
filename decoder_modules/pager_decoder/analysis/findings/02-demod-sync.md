# Stage 2 (sync/mode detection) + Stage 1 (symbol demodulation) — C vs C++ correctness comparison

## Verdict

The pure sync-detection and mode-decode logic is a **faithful, correct port**: the sync marker
(`0xA6C6AAAA`), the Hamming-distance threshold (`< 4`), the `count_bits` popcount algorithm, the
64-bit shift-register bit order (`<<1 | (sym<2?1:0)`), the outer-code XOR check, the polarity
(normal/inverted) handling, and the `decode_mode` → (baud, levels) lookup table (all five sync
codes 0x870C/0xB068/0x7B18/0xDEA0/0x4C7C) match the C reference exactly. The `buildSymbol` DC-offset
IIR, zero-crossing PLL, majority-vote quantizer, and lock-pattern detector also match constant-for-constant.

**However there is one 🔴 correctness bug that defeats speed detection at the demodulator level:** the
C++ orchestrator calls `setBaudRate(1600)` unconditionally on **every** audio sample (FlexDecoder.cpp:131),
which clobbers the 3200-baud rate that is (correctly) programmed at the FIW→Sync2 transition. Because
`buildSymbol` derives its symbol-timing `phase_rate` from `current_baud_`, the demodulator always runs at
1600-baud symbol timing. Modes A2 (0x7B18), A4 (0xDEA0) and A7 (0x4C7C) — the three 3200-baud modes — will
mistrack and fail to decode even though their sync codes are detected correctly. Two secondary omissions
(envelope-window cap, integrate-and-dump alternate slicer) exist but are quality/adjacent-stage concerns.

## Notes / non-issues checked
- `phase_max = 100 * sample_freq`, `phase_rate = phase_max*baud/sample_freq`, `phasepercent = 100.0*phase/phase_max`, and the end-of-period `phase += phase_rate; if(phase>phase_max) phase-=phase_max` are identical (C:4645-4647,4742-4751 vs FlexDemodulator.cpp:42-45,79-84). The `(int64_t)` casts on the phase-error correction in C++ (:152,154) match C's implicit truncation into an integer `phase`.
- FLEX_SYNC_MARKER=0xA6C6AAAA identical (C:152 / FlexTypes.h:12).
- The redundant per-sample `setBaudRate(1600)` at FlexDecoder.cpp:131 is the sole cause of SYNC-01; the FIW→Sync2 setter at :278 and the after-data reset (FlexStateMachine.cpp:274 `DataState::onExit`) are otherwise correctly placed to mirror C.
- Verified by `grep "Demodulator.baud"` on the C file: the only writers are C:4456 (→Sync.baud, FIW→Sync2), C:4633 (→1600 after data), C:4678 (→1600 in the `!locked` reset branch), and C:4881 (→1600 in `Flex_New` init); C:4646 is the reader. **C has no unconditional per-sample reset**, confirming that removing FlexDecoder.cpp:131 is the correct fix and that FlexStateMachine.cpp:274 (DATA→Sync1) plus the `!locked` branch (FlexDemodulator.cpp:62) already re-establish the 1600 default exactly as C does.
